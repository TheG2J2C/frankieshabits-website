Privacy policy

Your habits are between you and Frankie.

Effective 26 July 2026

The short version: everything you do in Frankie's Habits stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. We couldn't read your data even if we wanted to — we never receive it.

What the app stores, and where

Frankie's Habits stores the habits you create, the activity you log, your goals, and Frankie's state (health, mood, bones, cosmetics, and so on) in a local database on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It is included in your normal device backup if you have iPhone backups enabled, which is handled by Apple under your own iCloud or computer backup settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no server of ours to send anything to. We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports of our own, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or health data from other apps.

Subscriptions

The optional adoption subscription is processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details. Apple provides the app with only an anonymous confirmation that a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device by the app. No notification content passes through our systems — we don't have any systems for it to pass through.

Emailing us

If you email support@frankieshabits.app or hello@frankieshabits.app, we'll have whatever you choose to send us — your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to reply, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and all of its data is deleted with it. Because we hold no copy, deletion is immediate and complete (and, fair warning, unrecoverable — this includes Frankie).

Children

Frankie's Habits does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — the app simply has no collection mechanism.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, if we add optional cloud sync one day — we'll update this page and the effective date above before that version ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@frankieshabits.app.
